⚠️ Before You Start

This tool itself does not record, save or transmit any audio. Transcription is performed by your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome/Edge, that means your browser sends audio to the browser vendor's cloud speech service to generate text in real time — this is a function of the browser, not of this app. This app never stores, uploads, or has access to that audio or any recording of it.

  • Transcripts are not verbatim legal records and may contain errors — they should not be used to quote, attribute statements to, or hold any individual accountable for exact wording.
  • Speaker labeling is manual/approximate, not verified voice identification — do not treat speaker attribution as definitive.
  • This is an internal, unofficial productivity aid — it is not a sanctioned system of record and must not be shared externally or used as evidence in any formal, legal, HR, or compliance context.
  • All transcript and notes data is cleared automatically when you refresh or close this page — nothing persists between sessions.
  • The "Send to Meeting Summarizer" button opens a MatchaOS mission in a new tab and copies your transcript only (not your notes) to your clipboard for you to paste in manually — it does not transmit data automatically.
  • Ensure all meeting participants are aware that notes are being taken, per your organization's meeting and recording policies.
